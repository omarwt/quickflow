# FE-09 Page transitions and navigation feel

Setup (done by `phase-09-seed.sh` on a fresh backend): the FE-07 data (tasks, habit "Morning run", cards
"Spring in Action" and "Rust course", plans), plus eight "Filler task" tasks. Use a 1280x800 window, the light colour scheme and
`prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` unless a step says otherwise. Use `browser_evaluate` for the measurements.

1. Open `/tasks`. Expected: `document.title` is "Tasks · QuickFlow". `typeof document.startViewTransition` is "function". The computed `view-transition-name` of `.sidebar` is "shell-nav" and of `main` is "page".
2. Evaluate `performance.getEntriesByType('resource').filter(e => e.name.includes('/api/habits')).length`. Expected: 0. Hover the "Habits" navigation link (`browser_hover`), wait 1 second and evaluate the same expression again. Expected: at least 1 (the data was fetched on hover, before any click).
3. Evaluate `window.__vt = 0; const s = document.startViewTransition.bind(document); document.startViewTransition = (cb) => { window.__vt++; return s(cb) }; window.__nav = document.querySelector('.sidebar')`. Click "Habits". Expected: the URL is `/habits`. Right after the click, the page shows the "Morning run" card and **no** loading skeleton (`document.querySelectorAll('.skeleton').length` is 0). `window.__vt` is 1. `window.__nav === document.querySelector('.sidebar')` is true (the navigation element was kept, not re-created). [screenshot]
4. Expected on `/habits`: `document.title` is "Habits · QuickFlow", `document.activeElement` is the `h1` with the text "Habits", and `window.scrollY` is 0.
5. **Keyboard only.** Press Tab until the "Todo Plans" navigation link is focused, then press Enter. Expected: the URL is `/plans`, `document.activeElement` is the `h1` "Todo Plans", and `document.title` is "Todo Plans · QuickFlow". Press Tab once. Expected: the "Create Plan" button is focused (focus continues from the heading into the page).
6. Resize to 390x700 and open `/tasks`. Scroll to the bottom (`window.scrollTo(0, document.body.scrollHeight)`). Expected: `window.scrollY` is greater than 0. Click "Learning Resources" in the bottom navigation. Expected: the URL is `/learning`, `window.scrollY` is 0, and the heading "Learning Resources" is visible.
7. Still at 390x700 on `/tasks`, click "Add Task". Expected: the dialog's computed `animation-name` includes "sheet-up". Press Escape. Resize back to 1280x800, click "Add Task" and read the dialog's computed `animation-name`. Expected: "dialog-in". Press Escape. [screenshot]
8. Switch to reduced motion (`browser_emulate_media` reducedMotion "reduce"). Evaluate `window.__vt = 0`, then click "Habits". Expected: the URL is `/habits` and the heading is "Habits", `window.__vt` is still 0 (no view transition under reduced motion), and focus is on the `h1` "Habits". Click "Add Habit". Expected: the dialog's computed `animation-name` is "none". Press Escape.
9. Switch back to no-preference and reload `/tasks`. Expected: `document.title` is "Tasks · QuickFlow". Focus is **not** moved to the heading on a first load (`document.activeElement` is `document.body`), and pressing Tab once still focuses the "Skip to content" link.
