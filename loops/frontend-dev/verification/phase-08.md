# FE-08 Responsive layouts for all screen sizes

Setup (done by `phase-08-seed.sh` on a fresh backend): tasks "Write report", "Pay bills" and "Call dentist", habit
"Morning run", learning cards "Spring in Action" and "Rust course", plans "Yesterday review" and "Soon". Resize with
`browser_resize` and measure with `browser_evaluate` (`getBoundingClientRect()`, `document.documentElement.scrollWidth`).
Light colour scheme.

1. Resize to 320x640 and open `/tasks`. Expected: `scrollWidth` is at most 320. The bottom navigation has six links (accessible names Dashboard, Tasks, Habits, Learning Resources, Todo Plans and Settings), each fully inside the viewport, and the visible label "Dashboard" isn't cut off (the label element's `scrollWidth` isn't larger than its `clientWidth`). A "Filters" button is shown and the "Status filter" select isn't visible. The "Write report" row has buttons with the accessible names "Edit", "Archive" and "Delete", each at least 24x24 px. [screenshot]
2. Still at 320x640, click "Filters". Expected: a dialog "Filter tasks" docked to the bottom (its bottom edge within 2 px of the viewport bottom, and its width equal to the viewport width). It contains the Status, Priority, Due date and Sort selects and "Show archived". Choose Priority "Medium" and click "Show tasks". Expected: the dialog closes, the button reads "Filters (1)" and the three tasks are still listed. Open "Filters" again, set Priority back to "All priorities" and click "Show tasks". Expected: the button reads "Filters". [screenshot]
3. Still at 320x640, click "Add Task". Expected: the dialog is docked to the bottom and the Title field has focus. Type a title of 120 characters with no spaces (e.g. "A" repeated 120 times) and press Enter. Expected: the dialog closes, the task is listed and `scrollWidth` is still at most 320. [screenshot]
4. Resize to 844x390 (a phone in landscape) and open `/habits`. Expected: `scrollWidth` is at most 844. The navigation is a vertical rail on the left, at most 80 px wide and fully visible. The "Habits" heading and the "Morning run" card are visible. Click "Add Habit". Expected: the dialog fits inside the 390 px height (scroll inside it if needed) and its "Add habit" button can be reached. Press Escape.
5. Resize to 768x1024 and open `/learning`. Expected: the navigation rail is at most 80 px wide, shows icons only, and its links keep their accessible names. Record the left edge of `main`. Click "Expand navigation". Expected: the navigation shows the text "Learning Resources" and is at least 200 px wide, and the left edge of `main` hasn't moved (the rail opens over the content). Press Escape. Expected: the rail collapses. Click "Expand navigation" again, then click "Todo Plans". Expected: the URL is `/plans` and the rail is collapsed. [screenshot]
6. Resize to 1024x768 and open `/plans`. Expected: the sidebar shows the text labels (e.g. "Todo Plans" is visible) and no "Expand navigation" button is visible.
7. Resize to 1440x900 and open `/tasks`. Expected: in the "Call dentist" row, the title and its "Todo" badge are on the same line (their bounding-box tops differ by less than 12 px). [screenshot]
8. Resize to 1920x1080 and open `/plans`. Expected: the `main` element is at most 1320 px wide and centred in the space to the right of the sidebar (the gaps on its left and right differ by less than 40 px). [screenshot]
9. Resize to 640x800, the layout of a 1280 px window at 200% zoom, and open `/settings`. Expected: `scrollWidth` is at most 640, the bottom navigation is shown, and the "Save settings" button is fully visible after scrolling to it.
